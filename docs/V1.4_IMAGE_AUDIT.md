## Placement rules

- In every slot the reader should be able to answer: **What made this problem important? What option was rejected? What was selected and given up? What changed or was learned? What would be tested next?**
- The screenshot must answer a specific one of these questions; a generic hero illustration is not sufficient.
- The existing CTR `gift-9.png`, `gift-12.png`, `gift-13.png` and Catalina `music-7.png`, `music-12.png`, `music-14.png`, `music-15.png` remain. Do not duplicate them without conveying new evidence.
- Design-reconstruction visuals must be labelled accordingly and never described as a historical rejected prototype or working deployment.
- Do not invent persona participants, CTR denominators, AI data semantics, a release feature pair, source PRs, or model-evaluation outcomes.
- Crop consistently, preserving enough surrounding UI to understand the problem; no unnecessary phone mock-up frames or decorative backgrounds.
- **CTR card scroll specification:** preserve the product-list surroundings, use source Figma layers/frames and animate only the banner-card region; support reverse scroll, accessible control states, mobile layout and reduced motion. **This interactive implementation remains pending receipt of original Figma files.**
- Default visitor view hides all `.v14-image-slot` elements. Version editing mode is `?versions=1`; purple requests and their exact composition notes appear there.
- No merge of v1.2, v1.3 or v1.4 is authorised by this audit.
