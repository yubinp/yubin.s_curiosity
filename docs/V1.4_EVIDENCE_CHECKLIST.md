# v1.4 ★ — Evidence & Build Proof acceptance checklist

Based on v1.3, separate Draft PR. No merge to main. Version review: `?versions=1` (purple `v1.4 ★` markers). Normal reader view hides editorial notes.

## Mandatory decision narrative ★ (all cases)

- **1. Why this problem matters** — user or operational pain, business context and source status. Avoid assigning cause from a metric alone.
- **2. How alternatives arose; which was chosen and why** — genuine exploration or explicitly labelled present-day analytical comparison; chosen option, limitations, engineering constraints, and trade-offs. Never fabricate rejected historical prototypes.
- **3. What changed or was learned** — distinguish measured outcomes, shipped artifacts and hypotheses. Be explicit about unknown causality and user effect.
- **4. If I Did It Again** — an implementable proposed experiment with observable success criteria, data source and potential falsification. Never claim a future proposal was completed.
